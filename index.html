About Me

Hi! I'm Abi, a student at Purdue University majoring in Themed Entertainment Design. I have been involved in the creative process from a young age, growing up in Indiananapolis, IN where my dad owns an architectural woodwork and custom cabinetry business. He loved creating outside the box using unique and difficult designs, and would often clue me into his process - which began my love for creative solutions. After the pandemic, I became obsessed with social and themed events - planning a new experience to visit almost every month. When I found out there was an entire degree based around themed entertainment, I jumped at the chance to join! Since jopining the program, I've already learned so much about design, production and fabrication.
